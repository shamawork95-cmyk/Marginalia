# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 8, 2026**

Marginalia is built around one rule: your documents, your annotations, and
your reading are yours alone. This policy explains exactly what that means in
practice, because "everything stays on your machine" is a claim worth
checking rather than taking on faith.

## Who this policy covers

Marginalia is developed and operated by Shama Iqbal Hussain ("we", "us"). If
you have questions about this policy or how the app handles your data,
contact **marginaliaannotator@gmail.com**.

## What Marginalia does not do

- It does not require an account, sign-in, or email address to use.
- It does not collect analytics, usage metrics, or behavioural data.
- It does not use crash-reporting services.
- It does not send your documents, annotations, notes, or theme tags to any
  server — ours or anyone else's.
- It does not use cookies or trackers, because there is no web page or
  account system to attach them to.

## What is stored, and where

Everything you create in Marginalia — uploaded documents, the extracted
text, your highlights, notes, underlines, theme tags, and reading
preferences (your name, colours, palettes, typography, dark mode) — is
written to a folder on your own computer:

- On the installed desktop app, that folder lives in your operating
  system's standard per-user application-data directory, so it follows your
  own user account and is untouched when the app updates itself.
- You can see and change this folder at any time from **Settings → Storage**
  ("Show in Finder / File Explorer", "Change Folder…").
- Deleting a document from the library panel deletes it from that folder
  immediately and permanently. There is no server-side copy, backup, or
  trash to recover it from.

If whoever set up your installation configured an automatic retention
window, documents older than that many days are deleted by a local cleanup
task on the same schedule — this is off by default and, when on, only ever
deletes from your own local folder.

## The one network request Marginalia makes

The desktop app checks GitHub's release feed to see if a newer version is
available, either on launch or when you press "Check Now" in
**Settings → Updates**. That request carries only what is needed to find the
right release (your platform and the app's current version) — never your
documents, annotations, or anything else you have written. You can turn
these checks off entirely from the same Settings panel. When you choose to
install an update, the update file itself is downloaded from GitHub the same
way.

No other part of the app makes network requests. Opening, reading, and
annotating a document all happen entirely offline.

**Send feedback** opens a small dialog and sends nothing by itself. Its link to
the feedback form opens that form in your own web browser; the form is hosted
by Google (Google Forms), so what you type there goes to Google and to the
Marginalia team, under Google's terms. The dialog also shows our email
address, which you can copy or open in your own mail app.

## Children's privacy

Marginalia is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from anyone, of any age — the app simply has
nothing to send anywhere to collect.

## Changes to this policy

If what Marginalia stores or transmits ever changes, this document will be
updated and shipped with the app itself (Settings → Legal), not just posted
somewhere online you'd have to go looking for.
